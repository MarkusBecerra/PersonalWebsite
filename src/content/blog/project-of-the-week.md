Slab is honest. There's nothing to grab and nothing to blame — just friction,
balance, and your willingness to trust your feet.

## The problem

A high V5 with a blank mid-section: two smears, a long reach to a sloper, then a
delicate rock-over to finish. No power move bails you out. You either commit to the
feet or you come off.

> The hold is there. The doubt is the crux.

## What it taught me

Three sessions in, I stopped trying to *muscle* it and started trusting the rubber.
Sent it on the next go. Same lesson as always: slow down, breathe, commit.

Next up: a steep overhang that wants exactly the opposite of everything above.
