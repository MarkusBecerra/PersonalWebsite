There's a specific window — maybe forty minutes — when the sun is low and the room
softens. The crowd isn't peak-time yet, but they're warming. That's my favorite
slot to play.

## Reading the room

You don't fight the hour, you ride it. Open warm and patient, let the low end breathe,
and save the bigger moments for when the sky actually turns.

1. Start melodic, mid-tempo.
2. Layer percussion in long, telegraphed transitions.
3. Lift the energy as the light drops.

I'll post the full tracklist and a recording once it's mastered. The closing track
was a deep-house edit I've been sitting on for months — felt right to finally let it go.
